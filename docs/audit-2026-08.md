# What is missing — the August 2026 audit

Written 2026-08-10, after the immersive plan's built phases shipped. Eight
agents: four researching the live award-site bar (Awwwards/FWA 2025–26 winner
patterns, community tooling, top-studio information architecture, and a
fresh-eyes completeness inventory of this repo), three adversarial critics
(juror, motion director, IA critic), one synthesizer. The owner's prompt: the
site still doesn't feel dynamic, 3D, or multimedia enough, and the pages feel
incomplete. The audit's job was to find out whether he was right.

**He was. Honest composite score: 5.2–5.4 against a 7.65 entry band**
(juror 5.4 — Design 6.0 / Usability 5.5 / Creativity 5.5 / Content 4.5;
motion director 5.3; architecture critic 4.5).

## The two findings that explain the feeling

**1. The 3D is a doormat, not a spine.** 61% of Q1-2026 SOTD winners carry 3D
through the whole page; scroll-driven 3D narratives outscore static showcases
by ~1.8 points. Our contour hero is exactly the pattern that stopped winning:
a gated, hero-only scene that scrolls out at the top — after which ~90% of the
scroll is a flat document. This is the literal mechanism behind "not dynamic,
not 3D." The fix is inversion: one persistent fixed canvas whose shader state
evolves across the already-shipped three acts (dense gold contours at night →
faint paper-grain topography in daylight → re-densifying into the finale),
driven by normalized scroll progress and velocity uniforms. Ported to an OGL
fullscreen triangle (~12KB replaces three.js's ~118KB) it is **net-negative
~100KB**. The act structure already shipped is the narrative skeleton; this is
a port-and-plumb job, not invention.

**2. "Pages feel incomplete" is literal — there are no pages.** Every nav
label except Contact is a same-page anchor; work and insights open modals with
no URLs; legal pages are anchors on contact.html; /lp/ is an orphan; workData
renders zero cards above dead modal machinery. A juror exhausts the site in
one scroll and two clicks. The cross-studio invariant (Clay, Locomotive, Obys,
Unseen, Active Theory) is work **two layers deep with a closed next-project
loop**. Minimum complete IA for a 2-case bilingual studio: `/work/` index,
two full case pages (redaction bars as art direction — the studied leaders
skip client metrics too, which neutralizes the constraint), `/lab/` for
self-initiated pieces (and the October awards piece later), real
privacy/terms, a designed 404, homepage cut to a trailer whose cards
*navigate*. The build pipeline for this already exists: build-pages.mjs
produces /lp/.

## Build order (≈8 weeks to the low 7s)

- **Phase 0 (done 2026-08-10):** safety-commit the uncommitted /lp/ workstream
  — it was the only copy.
- **Phase 1 — the wiring week:** Lenis; the honesty cleanup; reel re-encode.
  Upgrades everything already shipped before building anything new.
- **Phase 2 — type and tissue:** SplitText reveals + the EN/ES scramble;
  MPA View Transitions.
- **Phase 3 — the architecture:** /work/, two case pages, /lab/, legal, 404,
  trailer homepage, /es/ mirrors, sitemap; Services dropdown collapses;
  Insights promoted or deleted.
- **Phase 4 — the keystone:** hero ported three.js→OGL, persistent canvas
  across the acts; then distortion planes on case media, sharing the runtime.
- **Phase 5 — the mobile tier.** **Phase 6 — garnish:** per-act audio, cursor,
  frame-sequence reel.
- **Sept–Oct:** the authored WebGPU/TSL piece lands in /lab/ as the third case
  and carries the composite across the 7.65 band.

Every phase rides the deferred/gated tiers: the 247KB critical path and the
bilingual runtime swap are never touched, and total JS *decreases*.

## Standing notes

- Lenis, SplitText and a full OGL tree (with a working Flowmap distortion on
  /lp/) are already vendored and proven in this repo — the fastest path is
  wiring what exists, not adding dependencies.
- Skip curtains.js (unmaintained) and a second three.js context. Reserve
  WebGPU/TSL for the /lab/ awards piece, not the homepage.
- The showreel timecode is genuinely distinctive — keep it, make it never lie
  (reduced-motion gets a poster with the real duration).
