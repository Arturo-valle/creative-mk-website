Privacy Notice

Last updated / Última actualización: 2026-08-10

English

What we collect

  • Contact and intake forms: the name, email, and project details you type, plus the campaign parameters (UTM) and referrer of your visit, so we know how you found us.
  • Newsletter: your email address.
  • Concierge conversations: what you write to the intake assistant, so a person can pick up where it left off.
  • In your browser only: your language choice and an anonymous session id, kept in localStorage. They never identify you.

What we do not do

  • No advertising pixels run on this site.
  • No data is sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No cookies are used for tracking.

Where it lives

Form and conversation data is processed and stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure (Workers and D1), which also serves this site. Bot protection on forms is provided by Cloudflare Turnstile.

Your rights

Write to hey@creativemk.net to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We answer, and we delete.

Tus derechos

Escribe a hey@creativemk.net para saber qué tenemos sobre ti, corregirlo o borrarlo. Respondemos, y borramos.
