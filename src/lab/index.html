The lab

The lab: what the studio builds for itself, in public.

Client work carries names and numbers. Lab work carries neither — it is the studio using its own tools on itself, and everything here runs live on this site.

Why a lab exists at all

The publishing rule that governs client work — a name and an agreed figure, or it does not go up — is the right rule, and it has a cost. It means a young studio can be doing its best work and have almost nothing to show, because the best work belongs to someone who has not cleared it yet. The lab is the answer to that. There is no client to ask, so the constraint disappears and the technique can be shown at full strength.

One condition keeps it honest: nothing counts as a lab piece until it runs in production. Not a prototype, not a demo reel, not a shot of something that worked once. Both pieces below are load-bearing parts of this site — one answers your message, the other is the ground you are reading on — so what you can judge is not whether the studio can build them, but whether they hold up while you use them.
