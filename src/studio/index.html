The studio

A studio of one senior operator, and specialists when the work calls for them.

The honest limits of a studio this size

One operator is an advantage until it is a constraint, and it is worth naming both. The advantage: the person who reads your brief is the person who does the work, so nothing is lost in a handoff between a strategist who sold it and a designer who inherited it, and a decision made on Tuesday is in the build on Wednesday. The constraint: this studio runs few projects at once, on purpose. When the calendar is full you are told the date rather than sold a slot and queued behind it.

Two things exist so the work does not depend on one person being available. Everything is versioned and documented — the repository, the deploy path, the handover notes — so another engineer can pick it up without an archaeology phase. And every engagement ends with what it takes to run without us: an editing session, notes on what runs where, and a system that a business operates rather than rents.

What the studio builds for itself

The intake agent that answers on this site and the WebGL field behind the homepage are both ours, both live, and both documented. They are the most honest proof a one-person studio can offer: not a description of the work, but the work, running where you can poke it.
